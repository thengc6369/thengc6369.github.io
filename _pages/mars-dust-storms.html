Regional dust storm formation

Using atmospheric reanalysis data, I investigated the dynamical precursors of Mars' large regional dust storms, particularly those initiated by cross-equatorial 'flushing' storms. Our analysis revealed that strong northern midlatitude wave-3 disturbances and amplified thermal tides consistently precede these events. We also found differences between storms originating through the Acidalia–Chryse and Utopia–Isidis corridors, with the former often requiring multiple high-amplitude wave events and the latter capable of developing more abruptly.
