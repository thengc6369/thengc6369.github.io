I am a planetary atmospheric scientist studying the climate and atmospheric dynamics of various planets, including Earth, Mars, and Venus.

Currently, I am a Research Associate at the HUN-REN ELTE Theoretical Physics Research Group, where my work primarily focuses on atmospheric transport on Venus.

<b>Research</b> <br>
My research spans atmospheric and climate dynamics on various planets in the Solar System. I investigate these using a range of approaches, including global climate model (GCM) simulations, simplified dynamical (e.g., quasigeostrophic) models, and analysis of reanalysis and observational datasets. Below is a brief list of my past and current research interests. More details are provided on the respective planet pages.
