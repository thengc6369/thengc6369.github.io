Aphelion cloud belt variability and tropical wave dynamics

My research investigates the variability of Mars' tropical water-ice clouds and their interactions with atmospheric waves. Using Mars GCM simulations, we demonstrated that radiatively active water-ice clouds fundamentally modify the tropical wave spectrum, favouring particular eastward- and westward-propagating modes. Cloud radiative feedbacks also play an important role in sustaining the large-scale Tropical Cloud Oscillation (TCO), characterized by shifts in cloud abundance between Tharsis and Syrtis Major. The GCM results suggest that the Tharsis-Syrtis cloud shifts correspond to transitions between quasi-equilibrium climate states.

More recently, we developed an observation-based Aphelion Cloud Belt index to quantify these shifts. Our results suggest that the first major transition each Martian year is seasonally controlled, whereas subsequent transitions are more irregular.
